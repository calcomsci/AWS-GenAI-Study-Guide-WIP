This is a study guide for the Generative AI Developer - Professional, AIP-C01, at AWS.

Warning: the contents of the exam is, as the name suggests, at professional level. It assumes that you have used AWS in the past (AWS recommends 2+ years of experience with the AWS console) and understand the core concepts of computer science (I have studied computer science. The actual, or even estimated, amount of years required to understand the average AWS question is at an equivalent 2+ years as AWS).  

The entire repository contents can be found on the Github link below. You don't have to download or deploy anything! Just visit the page below.
https://calcomsci.github.io/AWS-GenAI-Study-Guide-WIP/

The validity of the guide contents itself is in the verification phase. While I used generative AI extensively to generate the content in the guide, a human still needs to verify that its contents actually work. It took me about two months to prepare for the first mock exam, in which I passed. The bare minimum to pass the exam is 750/1000 score, or roughly 59-60 questions answered correctly. The exam contains 10 ungraded answers. That means out of the 75 questions, you must answer 59 answers correctly (75 * 0.75 = 52.25, rounded up to 57). Since you are not supposed to know which of those 10 questions are ungraded, the bare minimum to pass a 75 question exam and be able to score a 750/1000 or more is 59 correct answers.
